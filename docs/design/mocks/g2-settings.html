5
First screen
One of only two places the voice is allowed to be warm. It says what the app does and what it refuses to do, and it does not promise a result.

Write down what you ate

One line is enough. The app works out which foods you meant, adds them up, and writes the day to Health.

It does not score your diet, set you a target or tell you what to eat. It records, and it reports.

Ten thousand foods are already on the phone
No account, and nothing needs a network

6
Before the Health sheet
iOS shows its permission sheet once per type, for ever, so this screen has to do the explaining first. It names all eight, says what they are for, and offers a way past.

What goes into Health

Eight figures per item, under your name as the source, so the Health app's own nutrition picture is filled in and the Longevity tab has something to read.

Energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat
Saturated fat, fibre, sugar, sodium

iOS will ask next, once per figure. Decline any of them and the app carries on as a local log, writing whatever it was allowed.

Nothing is read back out of Health except other apps' nutrition on the day you are looking at, and that is shown separately and never edited.

You can start this again from Settings at any time.
