The faces
System rounded for titles, figures and heads; system sans for body and captions. Nothing downloaded, nothing licensed, and it scales with Dynamic Type for free. The tab bar is icons, each carrying its name for VoiceOver.
What it buys
The brand kit already reserved this face for the wordmark and the energy figure. This branch is that decision taken seriously rather than widened by accident, and it is warm without a single decorative element.
What it costs
It looks like a very good iOS app rather than a thing of its own, and it is only itself on Apple hardware — the fallback elsewhere is an ordinary sans.
